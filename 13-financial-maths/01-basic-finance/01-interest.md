### Definition of interest

''' 
Interest is a fee paid by one party for the use of assets of another.

'''

Suppose you have 10,000 ETB, but your friend needs money to start a small business.

Your friend asks to borrow the money for one year and promises to return 10,800 ETB at the end of the year.

You agree.

After one year, your friend returns the money as promised.

Your financial situation is:

Original amount lent = 10,000 ETB
Total amount returned = 10,800 ETB
Additional money received = 10,800 − 10,000 = 800 ETB

That additional 800 ETB is called **interest**.

The original 10,000 ETB is called the **principal**.

Therefore,

'''
**Interest = Final amount − Principal**
'''
Interest = 10,800 − 10,000 = 800 ETB.

Why did your friend pay you the extra 800 ETB?

Because your friend had the opportunity to use your money for one year. You gave up the opportunity to use that money yourself during that period.

**Interest is the compensation for allowing someone else to use your money.**
'''
If you deposit money in a bank, you may receive interest.
If you borrow money from a bank, you generally pay interest.
'''

---
Important idea: Interest is connected to both money and time. The amount of interest earned or paid depends on factors such as the principal, interest rate, time, and method of calculating interest.
